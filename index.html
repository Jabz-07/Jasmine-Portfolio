Who I Am

About Me

Teaching was not my first career choice — I was initially drawn to the medical field. However, over time I developed a genuine passion for education, especially in working with children. This led me to pursue a degree in Special Needs Education, which has deepened my understanding of inclusive and learner-centered teaching.

"Every learner is unique and capable of growth when given the right support and opportunities. Education should be inclusive, compassionate, and responsive to the needs of all students."

As a future educator, I aim to create a learning environment where no student is left behind and where each learner is encouraged to reach their full potential. I integrate digital tools into my practice to support engaging and effective learning experiences.
